How to share

Join the gallery.

  1. Open the submission form and attach one JPEG or PNG of at most 10 MiB. We resize and compress it to under 1 MB for the gallery.
  2. Add an optional display name, figure description, feedback, citation, DOI and project link. Leave the name blank to display “Anonymous”. Your GitHub username and issue remain public.
  3. Confirm that you have permission to publish the image. A pull request is prepared for review; the figure appears after it is merged and the site is published.

The gallery keeps up to 200 approved figures, removing the oldest in reviewed pull requests. Deleted files remain in Git history, and the original attachment remains on the public issue. Do not upload unpublished or sensitive material.
